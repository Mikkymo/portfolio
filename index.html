About Me

Physics Graduate.
Data Analyst.

My journey into data started in a Physics lecture hall. Physics taught me to test assumptions, work with imperfect information and explain quantitative results clearly.

Today I apply those same habits to business data — building dashboards, writing SQL queries, and constructing Python analyses that help organisations understand what's happening and why. My work has spanned six areas: sales, CRM, healthcare, telecommunications, finance and supply chain.

I believe good data analysis is ultimately about communication. A chart no one understands is just noise. I build things people can actually use to make decisions.
